# Hosted service terms — review draft

**Not published or in force. No effective date assigned.** This is a reviewable product/commercial draft, with unresolved factual and contractual items below. It does not establish a customer agreement. Read the [review packet](README.md), [SRD](../SRD.md) and accepted [decisions](../DECISIONS.md) before using it.

## Draft wording based on accepted policy

Flockletter is an independent hosted mailing service for small US businesses. The intended operator is Frosty Flamingo Software, LLC; the owner has confirmed the Colorado DBA registration for Flockletter. Final contracting and contact details remain subject to factual verification.

An authorized workspace owner administers the subscription. Workspace access, subscription access, sender readiness and permission to send are separate. A payment, account creation or preview does not authorize a campaign. Sending requires the applicable verified sender, permission-to-contact evidence, current consent/suppression checks, approved campaign review, available allowance and provider/trust eligibility. The [acceptable-use draft](ACCEPTABLE_USE_DRAFT.md) has separate unresolved policy items.

The accepted monthly catalogue is below. Names remain working labels. USD amounts are 1500/2500/3900 integer minor units. Final tax treatment and presentation require verification; this draft does not assert a registration or configured tax collection.

| Shared workspace allowance | Starter | Growth | Pro |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly USD price | $15 | $25 | $39 |
| Active contacts | 2,500 | 5,000 | 10,000 |
| Maximum recipients per campaign | 2,500 | 5,000 | 10,000 |
| Campaigns per UTC calendar month | 2 | 5 | 20 |
| Recipient emails per UTC calendar month | 5,000 | 25,000 | 100,000 |
| Users | 1 | 3 | Unlimited |
| Companies / brands | 1 / 1 | 3 / 3 | 5 / 5 |

Hosted plans include delivery. Allowances are pooled across the subscription's companies and brands; all caps apply together. Twenty Pro campaigns does not promise twenty sends to 10,000 recipients. Real accepted test emails consume recipient-email allowance. Changing plans or retrying work does not reset consumed or unresolved usage. No automatic overage billing is in scope. Remaining storage/contact/seat/domain counting definitions must be completed before publishing final entitlement language.

Ordinary hosted trials require a verified provider-held card and a separate explicit acceptance of the exact recurring monthly price and first-charge date. The 14-day trial includes two campaigns, one user, one company, one brand, 250 active contacts and 500 total recipient emails, including real tests. Limits span the complete trial, across month boundaries. Reopening an account or changing brands/plans does not renew eligibility. Cancel before trial end to avoid the first charge; access continues through trial end. A failed first charge follows the failed-payment policy.

Under D-049, upgrades take effect immediately with proration only after the selected provider's behavior has been verified. Downgrades and cancellation take effect at renewal/current service end. Existing data or members are not silently deleted to meet a downgrade; the customer receives a remediation path. Paid access continues through the paid period after cancellation.

A failed payment starts the past-due clock at the first failure. Sending and other paid mutations suspend on day six; ordinary workspace access closes on day thirty. Repeated failures do not restart that clock. Essential billing, public unsubscribe/suppression and authorized retention-window data access remain available through the approved paths. Successful payment before closure restores otherwise eligible access; recovery after closure requires authorized support before purge. Payment does not clear a separate sender or abuse hold. Actual provider behavior remains a release gate.

Refund requests receive manual review under the [refund draft](REFUND_DRAFT.md). This does not guarantee a refund or authorize any individual refund.

Ordinary canceled-account data follows the accepted 90-day retention policy, subject to the remaining lifecycle/copy/hold review. D-048 separately permits indefinite minimal keyed trial-owner/card/workspace eligibility history solely to enforce lifetime one-trial/no-refresh protection. This is pseudonymous personal data, not anonymous data, and excludes raw identities/card fingerprints, emails, card numbers, addresses and message/list content. The existing public privacy source describes that narrow exception; it does not settle other retention exceptions or verified erasure of all copies.

Email support uses the accepted support address, `support@frostyflamingosoftware.com`. Sensitive or uncertain matters require a person. No response-time or availability guarantee is established. FFA's separately audited Growth complimentary entitlement bypasses Stripe/card requirements; the accepted policy does not itself issue a grant or waive sending controls.

Flockletter's original source is AGPL-3.0-only, with third-party notices preserved. These hosted service draft terms do not amend that software license or require self-hosters to purchase a hosted subscription.
