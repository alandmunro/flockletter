# Refund information — review draft

**Not published or in force. No effective date assigned.** D-049 already accepts manual refund review. This draft proposes customer wording for that accepted position; it does not approve an individual payment, refund or live billing mode. See the [review packet](README.md) and [hosted terms draft](TERMS_DRAFT.md).

## Draft customer wording

Contact `support@frostyflamingosoftware.com` to request review of a Flockletter subscription charge. A person reviews refund and billing-dispute requests. Contacting support does not by itself authorize a refund, promise a particular outcome or change the subscription's cancellation state.

Do not email full card numbers, security codes, passwords or other secrets. Account-specific requests require verification of your authority. An incoming email address alone does not establish authority to change billing. Support automation cannot issue refunds or change subscriptions.

Cancellation stops future renewals at the accepted effective service end; paid access continues through the paid period and timely trial cancellation preserves access through trial end without the first charge. A cancellation request is separate from review of a past charge. Review the current authoritative status in Subscription and use verified support if it is unclear.

No automatic refund guarantee, blanket no-refunds rule, eligibility deadline, refund percentage, processing-time promise or refund fee is established in this draft. Any refund actually approved must be executed and reconciled through the authoritative payment provider; a browser return or drafted support response is not proof that funds moved.

## Remaining review and evidence

- Confirm exact final refund wording together with business/tax facts and the hosted agreement. Do not convert manual review into an unapproved no-refunds policy.
- Verify the selected provider's actual refund permissions, event handling and customer-visible confirmation process. Do not promise timing until supported by verified provider behavior and approved wording.
- Confirm the human handoff and account-verification route. No response target or review hours have been accepted here.
- Keep refund execution separate from copy approval. Approval of these words cannot authorize a specific refund or charge.

Applicable sources: D-049; FL-BIL-003/005/006, FL-SUP-001/002; FL-AT-009/010/035. A documentation review is not live billing verification or legal clearance.
