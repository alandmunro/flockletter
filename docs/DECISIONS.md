# Flockletter product decisions

Updated November 8, 2026. D-047 records the approved bounded test-send policy; D-046 records autonomous completion and production credential-setup authorization. Earlier decisions remain historical or apply within their stated scopes; implementation, activation policy and verified outcomes are distinct.

## Accepted decisions

**D-047 · Bounded single-recipient test sends.** The owner approved tests to exactly one existing confirmed contact in the selected campaign brand, with at most five test emails per workspace per UTC day shared across its brands. Accepted real test messages consume the workspace’s recipient-email allowance, including the applicable trial allowance, and do not consume a monthly campaign slot. Preserve all ordinary current-authority, service, sender, consent/suppression, provider, quota/hold and unknown-outcome controls. Require a separate immutable test review followed by explicit approval of the saved campaign version, sender, one recipient and timing; this never approves the full campaign. An arbitrary address or new signup cannot bypass confirmation. Pending/uncertain work cannot create a daily-cap loophole, and unknown outcomes require reconciliation rather than a speculative resend. This test-send workflow uses captures in staging; live delivery retains its separate configuration and activation gates. The decision accepts this bounded policy without resolving unrelated billing, retention, legal/tax or provider facts. Implementation and verification are recorded separately in [the test-send playbook](implementation/TEST_SEND.md).

**D-046 · Complete remaining production work.** The owner authorized continuing until the remaining Flockletter architecture gaps are resolved, making reasonable decisions without routine stops, and setting up Resend, Clerk and Render production credentials as required. Use practical parallel work and maximize appropriate local-model work with primary-assistant validation. Keep staging on Render Free and retain D-031’s owner approval for every individual move above USD 25; splitting a move does not avoid approval. Preserve other applications, existing product photos and commerce settings. This extends D-044 and supersedes earlier staging-only implementation limits for Flockletter. The goal is not evidence that the diagram is green: record actual verification and unresolved facts. The instruction does not accept the proposed lifetime keyed trial-record retention exception, select an unknown Stripe merchant account or establish tax/registration facts. Existing action-specific configuration, privacy, capacity and verification gates remain; this record does not enable mailing, charges, hosted purge or resource modes. [Production preparation and unresolved decisions](implementation/PRODUCTION_LAUNCH_PREPARATION.md)

**D-045 · Flockletter cookie choices.** The owner identified Frosty Flamingo Flockletter as FFF and requested the FFA cookie-consent behavior on its site. Adapt the independent accept/reject, saved-choice, expiry, privacy-notice and withdrawal pattern to Flockletter's React shell. This does not transfer FFA analytics IDs, commerce events, cookies, account data or third-party tracker activation. Flockletter currently has no optional website analytics/advertising providers. [Implementation and verification](implementation/COOKIE_CHOICES.md)

**D-044 · Release code parity.** The owner authorized bringing Flockletter production and staging up to parity with development and GitHub. Promote the tested application, administration, API/MCP and worker source tree, maintaining isolated production identity and persistence. Keep staging on inexpensive exercise infrastructure. This supersedes earlier staging-only deployment authorization for Flockletter; it does not authorize changes to other applications or automatically activate customer mail, charges, AWS provisioning or hosted erasure. Existing activation, capacity, privacy and external-verification requirements remain in force. Durable production credentials require the applicable issuance approval. [Release and verification checklist](implementation/RELEASE_PARITY.md)
