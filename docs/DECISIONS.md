# Flockletter product decisions

Updated November 10, 2026. D-052 adds separately gated test-only delivery. D-048/049/050 record the accepted minimal lifetime trial-history exception, billing defaults and FFA Growth complimentary policy. D-047 retains the bounded test-send policy and D-046 the completion/credential scope. Earlier decisions remain historical or apply within their stated scopes; policy, implementation, activation and verified outcomes are distinct.

## Accepted decisions

**D-054 · Animated media, SVG import and optional email confirmation.** On November 11 the owner requested animated images, SVG uploads and contact subscription without an email response. Accept GIF and animated WebP while preserving their bytes/frames under bounded decoding. Import self-contained static SVG through strict XML validation and PNG rasterization; never serve uploaded active SVG. Existing photos remain unchanged. Add an owner-controlled brand signup choice: double opt-in remains the default, single opt-in immediately records affirmative form consent without claiming mailbox verification. Authorized imports and explicitly selected pending contacts may subscribe based on an affirmative permission declaration, current consent policy, source and actor. A new separately granted `audience:confirm` capability is required for delegated confirmation/import promotion; existing write grants gain no authority. Suppression, brand isolation, quotas, exercise/live provenance, review and sending gates remain. Changing settings does not retroactively confirm contacts. This amends D-017 and FL-AUD-002 only within this scope; Clerk login verification is unchanged. No email send or billing activation follows from this change.


**D-053 · Complete and issue the protected production complimentary grant.** The owner selected the existing workspace for the FFA pilot and authorized completing its accepted D-050 Growth setup. Extend the staging-only release entry point with explicit production inspect/apply, bound to reviewed source/environment/database/role/host and exact workspace/brand/revision. Preserve runtime read-only access, current owner/operator verification, billing-history refusal, audit and safe retries/revocation. Verify in isolation and staging before production issuance. This does not activate Stripe, cards, mail, public billing or additional customer entitlements. Keep private targets and credentials outside the public repository. [Operation and evidence](implementation/COMPLIMENTARY_ACCESS.md)

**D-052 · Build a separate test-only delivery gate.** The owner authorized completing independently buildable work toward a first test letter while general mailing and live billing remain off. Add an explicit `tests` sending scope, independently approved at activation, using D-047's existing one-confirmed-recipient review, five-request UTC-day admission and recipient-email accounting. Broadcast review, approval, resume and unsubmitted worker work must be denied in this scope; already-submitted uncertain outcomes remain reconciliation-only. UI, HTTP, MCP, worker and SES transport must agree. Sender, service, consent, suppression, provider production access and other release controls are unchanged. Staging verifies captured mail first. This authorizes implementation and tested code promotion, not external sends, complimentary grants, provider activation, new credentials or paid subscriptions. [Test-only operation](implementation/TEST_ONLY_DELIVERY.md)

**D-051 · Bounded browser font policy and Clerk style exception.** For the reported ZAP rule 10055 findings, the owner selected an explicit same-origin-only font policy and documentation of the remaining inline-style exception. Override Helmet's inherited broad HTTPS/data font sources at the shared HTTP boundary. Preserve Clerk sign-in/account/workspace controls, editor and sandboxed email preview styling. The current Clerk components require inline styles; retaining `style-src 'unsafe-inline'` is an accepted, visible limitation, not remediation of that second alert. Do not replace Clerk or move permissions between style directives to conceal the warning. Review the exception when Clerk's supported styling requirements or the editor/preview integration changes. [Policy and validation](implementation/BROWSER_SECURITY_POLICY.md)

**D-048 · Minimal lifetime trial-eligibility history.** The owner explicitly accepted indefinite retention of minimal keyed HMAC owner/card/workspace consumed trial-eligibility history beyond ordinary 90-day tenant purge, solely for lifetime one-trial/no-refresh protection. Necessary namespace/key-tag, policy/version, intent, state and time metadata stay bound to those minimal claims. They are pseudonymous personal data, not anonymous data. Raw Clerk/workspace IDs, provider card fingerprints, email, card numbers, addresses and message/list content are excluded from this global exception. Ordinary tenant deletion remains unchanged outside this narrow scope. Unconsumed reservation eligibility still expires after 35 minutes; this is not a promise of physical deletion at minute 35. Stable keys and versioned policy cannot be reset to refresh eligibility. Actual configuration, accurate disclosure, provider/history baseline and protected signed exception/checkpoint/executor evidence remain separate release checks. No hosted purge or individual recurring agreement is authorized by recording this policy. [Accepted scope and evidence](implementation/PROPOSED_TRIAL_RETENTION_EXCEPTION.md)

**D-049 · Billing defaults.** The owner accepted the 14-day card-required trial with two campaigns, one user/company/brand, 250 active contacts and 500 total recipient emails over the complete trial, including real tests. Paid allowances use UTC calendar months; changing plans or retrying work does not reset consumed or unresolved usage. Immediate prorated upgrades require actual selected-provider/portal behavior to be verified first; downgrades and cancellation apply at renewal/current service end. Refunds require manual review, with no automatic guarantee or authorization of an individual refund. Failed payment suspends on day six and closes on day thirty from the first failure; repeated failures do not restart the clock, and essential billing, suppression and authorized retention-window access remain. Existing $15/$25/$39 monthly USD tiers remain. Actual account/catalogue/webhook/portal behavior, legal/tax facts, published terms/disclosures and each current customer's exact price/date acceptance remain required; policy approval is not a charge, subscription or live-mode switch. [Billing](implementation/CUSTOMER_BILLING.md), [production preparation](implementation/PRODUCTION_LAUNCH_PREPARATION.md)

**D-051 · Temporary pre-revenue operator authentication.** The owner explicitly requested removing production MFA until paid customers. Defer only the second-factor requirement under an explicit production-only `pre-revenue` policy; keep a signed Clerk session with first-factor verification younger than five minutes, the separate operator organization, exact named operators and current admin membership. All existing authorization, origin, role, case, audit and action gates remain. The exception refuses hosted retention or approval signing activation. Restore and verify MFA once paying customers arrive as an explicit owner follow-up; no revenue detector or billing prerequisite is imposed. Default policy remains `required`. No Clerk purchase, new access grant, provider activation or customer-login change follows. This amends D-043's MFA minimum for this bounded period. [Implementation and rollback](implementation/PRE_REVENUE_OPERATOR_AUTH.md)

**D-050 · FFA Growth complimentary policy.** The owner selected Growth for FFA’s 100% complimentary service through the existing Stripe/card-bypassing entitlement path. Its pooled subscription limits are five campaigns per UTC month, 5,000 active contacts and 5,000 recipients per campaign, 25,000 monthly recipient emails, three users, three companies and three brands. Actual issuance requires the exact verified FFA workspace and authorized operator, with reason, effective date and durable grant/revocation audit. This policy record does not claim a grant was issued or verified. A company name/domain cannot create entitlement; normal consent, sender, suppression, usage, hold and provider controls remain. Exact pilot recipients and operational activation remain separate. [Complimentary billing path](implementation/CUSTOMER_BILLING.md)

**D-047 · Bounded single-recipient test sends.** The owner approved tests to exactly one existing confirmed contact in the selected campaign brand, with at most five approved test requests per workspace per UTC day shared across its brands; failed, rejected or canceled tests do not refund that daily admission. Accepted real test messages consume the workspace’s recipient-email allowance, including the applicable trial allowance, and do not consume a monthly campaign slot. Preserve all ordinary current-authority, service, sender, consent/suppression, provider, quota/hold and unknown-outcome controls. Require a separate immutable test review followed by explicit approval of the saved campaign version, sender, one recipient and timing; this never approves the full campaign. An arbitrary address or new signup cannot bypass confirmation. Pending/uncertain work cannot create a daily-cap loophole, and unknown outcomes require reconciliation rather than a speculative resend. This test-send workflow uses captures in staging; live delivery retains its separate configuration and activation gates. The decision accepts this bounded policy without resolving unrelated billing, retention, legal/tax or provider facts. Implementation and verification are recorded separately in [the test-send playbook](implementation/TEST_SEND.md).

**D-046 · Complete remaining production work.** The owner authorized continuing until the remaining Flockletter architecture gaps are resolved, making reasonable decisions without routine stops, and setting up Resend, Clerk and Render production credentials as required. Use practical parallel work and maximize appropriate local-model work with primary-assistant validation. Keep staging on Render Free and retain D-031’s owner approval for every individual move above USD 25; splitting a move does not avoid approval. Preserve other applications, existing product photos and commerce settings. This extends D-044 and supersedes earlier staging-only implementation limits for Flockletter. The goal is not evidence that the diagram is green: record actual verification and unresolved facts. At that point the instruction did not accept the proposed lifetime keyed trial-record retention exception; D-048 subsequently accepts only its documented minimal scope. Neither decision selects an unknown Stripe merchant account or establishes tax/registration facts. Existing action-specific configuration, privacy, capacity and verification gates remain; this record does not enable mailing, charges, hosted purge or resource modes. [Production preparation and unresolved decisions](implementation/PRODUCTION_LAUNCH_PREPARATION.md)

This does not transfer FFA analytics IDs, commerce events, cookies, account data or third-party tracker activation. Flockletter currently has no optional website analytics/advertising providers. [Implementation and verification](implementation/COOKIE_CHOICES.md)

Promote the tested application, administration, API/MCP and worker source tree, maintaining isolated production identity and persistence. Keep staging on inexpensive exercise infrastructure. This supersedes earlier staging-only deployment authorization for Flockletter; it does not authorize changes to other applications or automatically activate customer mail, charges, AWS provisioning or hosted erasure. Existing activation, capacity, privacy and external-verification requirements remain in force. Durable production credentials require the applicable issuance approval.
