# Annual birthday and anniversary greetings

Owners configure each brand in **Audience → Birthday and anniversary automations**. Save and preview the draft, review the channel and local time, then confirm the annual greeting. Editing an active definition pauses it until the new revision is approved. The upcoming view covers 30 days. A cancellation is durable for this annual occurrence. The following year's greeting remains eligible.

The SMS template supports `{{firstName}}`, `{{label}}` and `{{years}}`. Wrap optional wording in `{{#years}}…{{/years}}` to omit the whole phrase when an anniversary year is unavailable. The preview substitutes “friend” for a missing first name. Dates without evidence of a source brand stay out of the scheduler. The controls show attributable legacy phone dates for explicit confirmation. Shared household phones are excluded from personalized SMS.

The SMS preview shows the first time allowed by both the greeting window and the effective operator texting hours, using the same recipient zones as the SMS worker. A greeting with no permitted SMS time is shown as held. Email fallback uses the greeting window.

The operator setting `annual_catch_up_days` controls how many local days after the occasion remain eligible. The platform starts at two. Operators may set zero through 30 and may override the platform value for a workspace. Changes are audited and apply to outstanding work at planning and before submission. A completed, cancelled or expired greeting keeps its receipt. After an outage, the worker resumes from its last complete scan and records eligible dates it missed outside the window as expired, including dates it had never queued. Dates first entered after the missed occasion do not create expired history.

## Release and capture verification

1. Merge the five changes in order. For each candidate verify the exact commit on main and its Foundation and self-host checks. Record the currently serving staging web and worker revisions. Rehearse each schema change against those revisions, then use the protected staging migration runner and the repository's staging deployment workflow. Verify the deployed version and readiness for both processes. [Release operations](RELEASE_RUNNER.md)
2. Select `FLOCKLETTER_DATE_AUTOMATIONS=capture` on staging web and worker for exercise. Use mock texting, captured customer delivery, the private renderer and exercise billing. Capture activation requires the same mode at queue handoff and submission. Missing or unrecognized automation configuration selects off. Production rejects capture by selecting off. Use synthetic workspaces and disconnected test subscribers.
3. In the first workspace add an opted-in mock number, a confirmed email-only recipient, a shared phone and a stored February 29 birthday. Create a different brand in the second workspace. Preview and activate each date type, cancel one greeting and inspect history. Exercise the SMS callback and email capture, change a date, issue STOP, unsubscribe and revoke a connection before dispatch. Verify owner/member visibility and that a connection needs read and automation management for the brand plus the selected channel grants to activate. Compare stored receipts with mock Twilio acceptance and captured email. Inspect workspace operations for heartbeat and last successful pass.
4. To pause, disable each active definition. Set both processes to off to stop all new annual submissions. Retain receipts and let the channel workers reconcile requests that already reached a provider. A queued email uses one campaign slot and one email recipient allowance. SMS uses the ordinary marketing frequency, registration and segment controls. A hold or exhausted allowance never redirects an SMS to email. A definite delivery failure or unknown response stays on the selected channel.
5. Apply down migrations only after verifying rollback guards and preserving history. Rehearse restoration against a disposable database. Check the erasure receipt, personal-data inventory and suppression history before restarting a restored worker. For live activation use reviewed production channel settings and explicit owner pilot scope. Record expected dates independently of the scheduler for 30 days and reconcile each to a success, skip, expiry, cancellation or open provider investigation before wider rollout.
