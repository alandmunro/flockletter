# Birthday and anniversary automations

Implementation plan against `main` at `b306ad24e0b18cb960a8b56abaaa1dc6ee4ec08e`, November 25, 2026. The first release covers annual plain-text SMS and an optional marketing email fallback for birthdays and labeled anniversaries such as a home purchase. A workspace owner should be able to preview the next month, cancel one greeting, and explain from its history why any greeting was sent, deferred or skipped. The defaults proposed below still need to be recorded with the implementation. D-063 covers the earlier texting phase. Hosted activation of recurring marketing is a separate decision. ## Proposed scheduling and delivery decisions

- **Annual identity.** Use one persistent definition per brand and date type, with revisions for edits. Enforce uniqueness on workspace, definition, stable person/date identity and local occurrence year. Keep the scheduled civil date and UTC instant as editable attributes before submission. Moving a date, pausing and re-enabling, or editing a template cannot create a second greeting for the same annual event. Preserve identity through a resolved phone-to-subscriber link. If identity is ambiguous, withhold personalized SMS and surface the reason.
- **Calendar.** Compute in the recipient's IANA time zone. Use an explicit recipient zone first, then the existing phone area-code mapping, then the configured workspace zone. For ambiguous area codes use the workspace clock for the requested time and the intersection of candidate zones' allowed windows for SMS. Show the inferred zone in preview. Require an explicit workspace default at activation when it is needed. Move a nonexistent DST time to the first valid permitted instant and choose the earlier permitted instant for a repeated hour. Observe February 29 on February 28 in non-leap years. Render anniversary years from the original year and occurrence year, with an alternate phrase when unknown or less than one. Validate that a future source anniversary never yields a negative count. [Time-zone and send-window implementation](../../src/server/texting/messaging.ts)
- **Quiet hours and catch-up.** Apply the documented 9 a.m. to 8 p.m. local default and intersect it with stricter operator and tenant windows. The existing operator settings alone do not implement tenant narrowing. Display the effective send time. Recover occurrences within two local calendar days after the intended date by default, subject to the next permitted window. Mark older work expired with a reason. Activation and resume start prospectively, so an outage is the only source of catch-up. [Texting requirements](PROPOSED_TEXTING_AND_GIFTING.md) · [Phase 2 implementation gaps](TEXTING_PHASE_2.md)
- **Edits and cancellation.** Materialize preview records so cancellation survives regeneration and restart. Recalculate eligible unsent rows on date or template changes, and require approval of changed content before it can send. Recheck the date, enabled revision, authority and cancellation at the final submission claim. Clearing a date removes it from future dispatch. Once a provider call can have started, show that cancellation is too late. Freeze the approved content, provider route and any future offer reference and finish reconciling that submission.
- **Channel choice.** Default to SMS. The owner may enable a separately reviewed email template. Select email only when SMS lacks recipient eligibility before submission and the same person has current eligible email for the brand. A phone STOP governs that SMS relationship. A brand email unsubscribe governs email. A global hold, quiet hours, throttling or a provider outage must not redirect traffic to bypass a gate. Once a provider might have received a request, lock its channel and reconcile. An undelivered SMS never triggers an automatic second greeting under this proposal.
- **Scope beyond the first release.** DATE-1 also calls for multiple custom dates per person. The current type check and unique indexes support one birthday and one anniversary. Keep occurrence identity tied to a date record so a later additive migration can support recurring custom dates with distinct IDs and labels. WA-06-R05 also covers immutable offer identity across channels. Reserve the integration contract now; issuance, redemption and gift funding depend on WA-08/09. Track those deliverables separately from birthday and anniversary plain-message completion. [WA-06 dependencies](../supplemental-requirements/specs/06-date-automations.md)
