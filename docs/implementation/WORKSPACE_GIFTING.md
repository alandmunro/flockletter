## Reconcile a claim or payment

1. **Claim.** Confirm eligibility and the reviewed product set against `GET /products?country=US&currency=USD`. Persist intentional claim before submission. Read `GET /orders/{offer_id}` by external ID before attempting `POST /orders` with the same offer ID and immutable terms. On timeout, conflict, outage or review, hold the reservation and look up again. On verified `EXECUTED`, match recipient, value, currency, order and LINK reward before consuming credit once. Inspect failed/canceled attempts by provider order ID. A revisit uses `POST /rewards/{id}/generate_link`. Earlier links remain valid. Keep links and claim tokens out of evidence. [Create](https://developers.tremendous.com/reference/create-order) · [Lookup](https://developers.tremendous.com/reference/get-order) · [LINK delivery](https://developers.tremendous.com/docs/link-delivery)
2. **Events.** Tremendous permits one HTTPS receiver per organization, configured with `POST /webhooks`. Preserve any existing receiver. Verify the raw-body `Tremendous-Webhook-Signature`, durably deduplicate by `uuid`, acknowledge with 200, then retrieve the resource. Watch order approval/cancellation, top-up credit/reversal and funding events. Single-reward API failures may be synchronous. LINK rewards have no delivery-success webhook or proof of store redemption. [Webhook API](https://developers.tremendous.com/reference/create-webhook) · [Events and retries](https://developers.tremendous.com/docs/webhooks-1)
3. **Stripe test scope.** At `https://api.stripe.com/v1`, verify `GET /account` against the expected test account. Its test key needs account and charge reads, PaymentIntent creation/confirmation and lookup, and refund creation/lookup. The fixture uses `POST /payment_intents` and `POST /payment_intents/{id}/confirm`. Match `GET /payment_intents/{id}` to the purchase, USD, received amount and `livemode=false`. For closure, look up `GET /refunds?payment_intent={id}` before `POST /refunds`. Match `GET /refunds/{id}` and `GET /charges/{id}` to the original payment. A workspace Checkout journey also needs its paid `GET /checkout/sessions/{id}` binding. Verify raw-body `Stripe-Signature` with the test endpoint’s signing secret and deduplicate event IDs. Exercise duplicate and out-of-order payment, refund and dispute events. Retrieve `GET /disputes/{id}` before changing disputed credit. The fixture confirms settlement by API lookup. Signed event delivery needs its own evidence. Test money never moves between banks. ## Recurring gifts and recovery

An owner can attach a funded campaign by name or configure a fresh gift for every birthday or anniversary. The annual worker reserves credit in the same transaction as the delivery handoff. The occurrence ID binds the offer across retries and channels. The recipient report includes the claim time and expiry, and exports the campaign to CSV.

The Stripe gifting destination handles `refund.created`, `refund.updated`, `refund.failed` and `charge.refunded` in addition to checkout and dispute events. It retrieves current refunds on the bound charge, holds pending principal and records settled adjustments once. A confirmed terminal Tremendous order releases its reservation. A post-claim cancellation requires independent confirmation of the full provider refund. Eligibility also considers verified subscription and texting-setup payments. A brand-specific abuse case in the operator portal records probation decisions and releases a reviewed loss once the debt is resolved.

Automatic funding is opt-in. Use `sandbox_automatic` or `live_automatic` for `FLOCKLETTER_GIFT_FUNDING_MODE` and set `FLOCKLETTER_GIFT_TOPUP_LIMIT_CENTS` to the maximum approved draw. Sandbox requires its existing approved source. Production requires `FLOCKLETTER_GIFT_LIVE_FUNDING_SOURCE` and a verified USD balance account. Keep `live_readonly` until the owner verifies the bank source and settlement. Uncertain and partial transfers keep their original idempotency key. Rejected and reversed transfers require operator review before another draw.
