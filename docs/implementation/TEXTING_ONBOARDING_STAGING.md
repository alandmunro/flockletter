# Self-service texting in staging

D-065 and D-067 authorize staging enrollment for synthetic US businesses with an EIN, DBAs and sole proprietors. WA-05 and the WA-10 setup-payment contract cover the staged flow. Broader release evidence is tracked in [WA-05](../supplemental-requirements/specs/05-sms-onboarding.md) and [D-063/D-065/D-067](../DECISIONS.md).

## Practice enrollment

1. Use a synthetic staging workspace with permission to manage its company. Under Audience, enable a Flockletter public sign-up page for a brand in the company. Turn on the mobile number field in Texting settings.
2. Under **Texting → Set up business texting**, save fictional business and contact details. Use a matching business website, privacy policy, SMS terms and business email domain. The page checker reads the three public pages and the company’s SMS opt-in preview. Resolve any findings and select **Check pages again**. Review the generated campaign examples and the displayed texting plan. A business without its own domain can preview and publish staging practice pages with a public support contact. This opens an ownership review.
3. Request and enter a separate practice code for the email and phone. Sole proprietors also complete a mock carrier challenge. In capture mode, confirm the stated practice fee and submit once. The worker reconciles the workspace carrier account, advances the brand and campaign, reserves the number, and waits for a signed mock activation event. The customer can leave the page and return, or watch Companies and numbers update automatically.
4. Reload or open the workspace in another tab to verify the saved progress. A different workspace owner attempting the same synthetic EIN goes to review. The operator’s workspace-scoped Texting controls can request correction or review a fictional ownership letter for eligible cases. Download the generated practice letter and upload it for the same company and enrollment revision, or attach the generated example directly. The upload accepts only the exact fictional file. The review records its type, source and receipt. Carrier ambiguity and overdue registrations appear in the review queue.
5. Set the required sending and probation settings at workspace scope for a test. For the first broadcast, use a campaign example and fictional opted-in recipients. Observe that the remainder waits for the first cohort to deliver and for its observation interval to pass. An unsuccessful cohort pauses pending delivery. Probation exits after both the minimum age and delivered-broadcast threshold, or an audited operator decision.
6. To exercise the failure path, finish all signup confirmations and review the first broadcast. In the same workspace's operator page, confirm the carrier is mock, then choose **Fail next text** once. Confirm the reviewed broadcast as owner. After the observation interval, check **First broadcast** on the operator page. The first cohort should show a failed carrier attempt and the remaining texts should show pending under probation with zero carrier attempts.

With mock texting, staging defaults to `FLOCKLETTER_TEXTING_ONBOARDING=capture`. Use `off` to pause self-service. For loopback development, explicitly select `FLOCKLETTER_TEXTING=mock` and `FLOCKLETTER_TEXTING_ONBOARDING=capture`.

An isolated Stripe sandbox can use `FLOCKLETTER_TEXTING_ONBOARDING=stripe_test` with the test account, secret key and endpoint signing secret in `FLOCKLETTER_TEXTING_SETUP_STRIPE_ACCOUNT`, `FLOCKLETTER_TEXTING_SETUP_STRIPE_KEY` and `FLOCKLETTER_TEXTING_SETUP_STRIPE_WEBHOOK`. Configure Checkout session completion, expiration, asynchronous success and asynchronous failure events for `/api/v1/texting/stripe-setup-events`. Return to Texting to refresh the saved payment. After a verified expiration or payment failure, retry the same enrollment. Each replacement has a new attempt identifier. A delayed event for an earlier attempt cannot pay its replacement. Staging capture can run without Stripe credentials.

## Recovery and plan changes

The owner sees enrollment notices in Texting. Staging captures the email copies. A failed first cohort holds its remaining texts. Restarting after review closes that broadcast, cancels its unattempted remainder and requires a newly reviewed first cohort. A suspension requires an operator decision. The worker checks opt-outs and filtering against privately configured risk settings and checks both age and delivered volume before completing probation.

In capture mode the owner can manage a separate monthly practice texting plan, set a spend cap and cancel or reactivate texting. A change requiring a Standard campaign goes through mock brand and campaign review while the existing registration stays active. The simulated agreement records the selected plan and amount. Newsletter access has its own agreement.

## Carrier readiness

For a future live release, set up Twilio Event Streams for the appropriate subaccount and confirm the account token and the exact destination `/api/v1/texting/twilio/carrier-events`. A newly purchased live number remains reserved until a signed registration-success event matches its owning account, Messaging Service and phone number. A lost purchase response is reconciled by lookup. Attachment resumes using the saved provider number SID. Long-running registration and provider ambiguity require operator review.

The local mock exercises account provisioning, sole-proprietor verification, DBA identity and registration recovery. Real business, phone, domain and payer verification, approved handling of actual identity documents, provider acceptance of hosted pages and DBAs, signed carrier events on real numbers and live resubmission access still require a provider pilot. Record FL-AT-045 carrier approval and opted-in delivery before a live owner pilot.

## Evidence

On November 25, 2026, the fresh Juniper and Alder synthetic workspaces each submitted a registration to the mock carrier on hosted staging at `83cdf5c85fdc529bcdbad96fff93ab8e4e6f19b5`. With Texting left open, each **Companies and numbers** row advanced from **Provisioning** through **Brand pending** and **Campaign pending** to **Approved**, then showed its own mock number and **Ready to send**. Juniper reached approval about 23 seconds after submission and Alder about 28 seconds. Both runs used the worker's own responses throughout, with no page reload or manual status refresh. [Staging deployment](https://github.com/alandmunro/flockletter/actions/runs/45679350263)

For WA-05-R04, three fictional subscribers in each workspace opted in through its hosted signup form and confirmed by replying YES through the mock carrier. Both owners reviewed an approved campaign example for three recipients and three segments.

- **Juniper, first broadcast.** The owner confirmed the send at 19:25:41 UTC on `83cdf5c`. The operator view showed `canary`, and the usage meter rose by one segment during observation. Staging switched to `4b85917` at 19:26:17 UTC during the wait. After the interval, the meter showed all three broadcast segments used and zero in progress. The operator view showed `active`, confirming release of the remaining two texts under probation. The three signup confirmation segments were already on the meter before the broadcast. The workspace's temporary test settings were restored after verification.
- **Alder, first broadcast.** On November 25, 2026, the operator chose **Fail next text** on hosted staging at `8dcb42b`. The owner submitted the reviewed three-recipient broadcast at 20:19:57 UTC. At 20:20:09 UTC the mock carrier had failed the first text (error `30005`) on its only attempt. Both remaining texts were pending with zero carrier attempts. At 20:21:34 UTC, 85 seconds after the failure was observed and past the observation interval, the operator page showed probation `paused`. Both remaining texts were still pending with reason `probation_paused` and zero carrier attempts. The workspace was left paused. [Staging deployment](https://github.com/alandmunro/flockletter/actions/runs/45693608698)
