# Self-service texting in staging

D-065 and D-067 authorize staging enrollment for synthetic US businesses with an EIN, DBAs and sole proprietors. WA-05 and the WA-10 setup-payment contract cover the staged flow. Broader release evidence is tracked in [WA-05](../supplemental-requirements/specs/05-sms-onboarding.md) and [D-063/D-065/D-067](../DECISIONS.md).

## Practice enrollment

1. Use a synthetic staging workspace with permission to manage its company. Under Audience, enable a Flockletter public sign-up page for a brand in the company. Turn on the mobile number field in Texting settings.
2. Under **Texting → Set up business texting**, save fictional business and contact details. Use a matching business website, privacy policy, SMS terms and business email domain. The page checker reads the three public pages and the company’s SMS opt-in preview. Resolve any findings and select **Check pages again**. Review the generated campaign examples and the displayed texting plan. A business without its own domain can preview and publish staging practice pages with a public support contact. For EIN businesses this opens an ownership review. A sole proprietor without an EIN follows the separate carrier challenge.
3. Request and enter a separate practice code for the email and phone. Sole proprietors also complete a mock carrier challenge. In capture mode, confirm the stated practice fee and submit once. The worker reconciles the workspace carrier account, advances the brand and campaign, reserves the number, and waits for a signed mock activation event. The customer can leave the page and return, or watch Companies and numbers update automatically.
4. Reload or open the workspace in another tab to verify the saved progress. A different workspace owner attempting the same synthetic EIN goes to review. The operator’s workspace-scoped Texting controls can request correction or review a fictional ownership letter for eligible cases. Download the generated practice letter and upload it for the same company and enrollment revision, or attach the generated example directly. The upload accepts only the exact fictional file. The review records its type, source and receipt. Carrier ambiguity and overdue registrations appear in the review queue.
5. Set the required sending and probation settings at workspace scope for a test. For the first broadcast, use a campaign example and fictional opted-in recipients. Observe that the remainder waits for the first cohort to deliver and for its observation interval to pass. An unsuccessful cohort pauses pending delivery. Probation exits after both the minimum age and delivered-broadcast threshold, or an audited operator decision.
6. To exercise the failure path, finish all signup confirmations and review the first broadcast. In the same workspace's operator page, confirm the carrier is mock, then choose **Fail next text** once. Confirm the reviewed broadcast as owner. After the observation interval, check **First broadcast** on the operator page. The first cohort should show a failed carrier attempt and the remaining texts should show pending under probation with zero carrier attempts.

With mock texting, staging defaults to `FLOCKLETTER_TEXTING_ONBOARDING=capture`. Use `off` to pause self-service. For loopback development, explicitly select `FLOCKLETTER_TEXTING=mock` and `FLOCKLETTER_TEXTING_ONBOARDING=capture`.

An isolated Stripe sandbox can use `FLOCKLETTER_TEXTING_ONBOARDING=stripe_test` with the test account, secret key and endpoint signing secret in `FLOCKLETTER_TEXTING_SETUP_STRIPE_ACCOUNT`, `FLOCKLETTER_TEXTING_SETUP_STRIPE_KEY` and `FLOCKLETTER_TEXTING_SETUP_STRIPE_WEBHOOK`. Configure Checkout session completion, expiration, asynchronous success and asynchronous failure events for `/api/v1/texting/stripe-setup-events`. Return to Texting to refresh the saved payment. After a verified expiration or payment failure, retry the same enrollment. Each replacement has a new attempt identifier. A delayed event for an earlier attempt cannot pay its replacement. Staging capture can run without Stripe credentials.

## Unfinished forms, website retries and corrections

Before accepting a registration, the owner can choose **Save unfinished draft**. The draft stays encrypted in that browser. The recovery key is available to the current owner for the same workspace, company and enrollment revision. Drafts omit the EIN, verification codes and publication consent. Another tab must reload before replacing or discarding a newer save. Completing the form clears its browser draft. Clearing browser storage removes unfinished drafts. The accepted business details remain available on the server.

A signup prerequisite appears before the business form if the company's public signup or mobile field needs setup. Temporary page-read failures retry after one, five and fifteen minutes through the worker, including when Texting is closed. A disclosure finding or an exhausted retry budget needs a correction or a manual recheck. The worker can recover a check interrupted by a restart.

An operator returning a flagged application puts it in **Business details need correction**. The owner sees which details to review. Verification and submission require a newly saved revision. Private operator notes stay in the audit history. Both browser enrollment exercises run as release checks, including a reload of a private draft and a company isolation check.

## Private probation settings and fictional identity letters

The staging web service reads the complete approved mock policy from `FLOCKLETTER_TEXTING_PROBATION_POLICY`. Use a private JSON object keyed by the probation setting names in the operator console. The server checks the full set, safe bounds, increasing rate thresholds and volume ramp. Existing audited workspace settings take precedence. An incomplete or inconsistent effective policy holds new sending. The operator page identifies private defaults without displaying their values. Carrier limits and the accepted plan allowance continue to apply independently. [Staging decision D-068](../DECISIONS.md#d-068--texting-probation-and-identity-evidence)

An owner can choose **Correct my application** on an unpaid, flagged enrollment before supplying evidence. Open payer disputes over a completed payment require reconciliation. Sole proprietors without an EIN verify their email, phone and separate carrier challenge. EIN ownership collisions cannot transfer an existing registration.

The staging web service accepts only the company- and revision-bound fictional IRS letter. Configure the primary reviewer and backup in that order as private, comma-separated Clerk user IDs in `FLOCKLETTER_TEXTING_EVIDENCE_REVIEWERS`. An empty seat grants no access. The named primary receives new cases. The backup can take responsibility for an exact case. Each view requires a fresh second factor and records the reviewer, workspace, company and revision. Accepting evidence also requires the assigned reviewer to have opened that original within five minutes. Operators can return an application for correction without viewing an original.

Originals expire seven days after the case closes or thirty days after upload, whichever comes first. To preserve an original for an open carrier appeal or legal hold, the assigned reviewer records the hold type and a review date within the next thirty days. The hold records its owner and remains in effect until released. An overdue hold remains visible for follow-up. A release after the ordinary deadline denies access immediately, and the worker removes the original. The audit receipt retains the document type, company, revision, reviewer, outcome and deletion observation. Published receipts omit file contents and the EIN.

The free staging instance stores fictional originals in a private temporary directory outside database backups. Worker passes reconcile the separate files against workspace receipts, including after a restore. Restoring a receipt cannot recreate a deleted original. Replacing the web instance can recover the identical fictional letter from its fingerprint and a separate, current database authorization. This preserves open cases and holds across web deployments. Purging revokes that authorization. A logical replay changes the database row version, and a database restart invalidates the earlier recovery generation. Every read requires a current authorization, even when the web instance still has a file. After a database restart or restore, the owner must attach a fresh fictional letter to an open case. A real-document launch requires separate durable private storage with preservation of holds, secure disposal from all storage copies, and an independently tested restore procedure.

## Recovery and plan changes

The owner sees enrollment notices in Texting. Staging captures the email copies. A failed first cohort holds its remaining texts. Restarting after review closes that broadcast, cancels its unattempted remainder and requires a newly reviewed first cohort. A suspension requires an operator decision. The worker checks opt-outs and filtering against privately configured risk settings and checks both age and delivered volume before completing probation.

The owner authorized one exception for Alder’s original three-text practice broadcast. On mock staging in capture mode, set `FLOCKLETTER_TEXTING_MOCK_BROADCAST_RELEASE` to the exact workspace ID and send ID, separated by a slash. The assigned workspace’s operator enters a reason and chooses **End practice probation and release the two held texts**. The server checks the original failed cohort, both unattempted message records and any competing sends or risk hold. In one transaction it records the operator, reason and all three message IDs, completes probation and wakes the two pending records for normal admission. An audit receipt prevents a second release. Clear the setting after verification. The customer’s ordinary restart still cancels the held remainder.

In capture mode the owner can manage a separate monthly practice texting plan, set a spend cap and cancel or reactivate texting. A change requiring a Standard campaign goes through mock brand and campaign review while the existing registration stays active. The simulated agreement records the selected plan and amount. Newsletter access has its own agreement.

## Carrier readiness

For a future live release, set up Twilio Event Streams for the appropriate subaccount and confirm the account token and the exact destination `/api/v1/texting/twilio/carrier-events`. A newly purchased live number remains reserved until a signed registration-success event matches its owning account, Messaging Service and phone number. A lost purchase response is reconciled by lookup. Attachment resumes using the saved provider number SID. Long-running registration and provider ambiguity require operator review.

The local mock exercises account provisioning, sole-proprietor verification, DBA identity and registration recovery. Real business, phone, domain and payer verification, approved handling of actual identity documents, provider acceptance of hosted pages and DBAs, signed carrier events on real numbers and live resubmission access still require a provider pilot. Record FL-AT-045 carrier approval and opted-in delivery before a live owner pilot.

## Automated enrollment and recovery checks

Texting identifies the missing signup prerequisite for the selected company. The owner can enable the optional mobile field in Texting or open Audience on the appropriate brand to publish its signup page. Subscriber consent remains a separate choice.

For mock capture, configure the private `FLOCKLETTER_TEXTING_MOCK_REGISTRATION_POLICY` JSON object on staging. It takes the three `mock_sole_proprietor_daily_limit`, `mock_low_volume_daily_limit`, and `mock_standard_daily_limit` settings, plus `registration_poll_minutes`, `registration_review_minutes`, and `reconcile_settle_minutes`. Use the operator console for audited overrides. Configure polling to occur before the overdue-review interval. Removing the deployment setting restores the ordinary configured operator limits. Existing mock receipts and accepted agreements remain available.

After a captured plan and number activate, the workspace receives the plan's monthly segment allowance and the daily limit for its approved mock registration tier. A lower operator ceiling still applies, including a zero monthly allowance. Multiple active senders share the most restrictive applicable daily tier. A sender whose tier cannot be established needs an explicit operator limit. Number activation, probation, spend caps and opt-in are separate checks.

| Design case | What to verify with the mock carrier |
| --- | --- |
| ONB-4, ONB-5, ONB-8 | The owner confirms the legal name and sees the campaign description, signup flow, samples, and confirmation, HELP and STOP wording. Confirm the worker files the reviewed wording. Watch brand, campaign and number milestones on the open page. |
| IDV-3, IDV-4, WA-05-AT02 | Compare the synthetic payer's full address and name. A mismatch holds submission. Distinguish a different owner claiming the same EIN from a duplicate trade name under the same owner. An ownership collision cannot transfer an EIN. |
| ONB-3, ONB-10, WA-05-AT01 | After a missed notification, let the scheduled worker find the existing carrier decision. For a number, require the signed receipt bound to the original account, service and number. Repeated pending checks do not reset the wait shown in the operator queue. |
| ONB-7, REG-5 | Correct a rejected campaign through the customer form. Verify the mapped correction, the existing eligible campaign identity and one setup receipt across resubmission. |
| REG-4, ONB-2 | Once the number is active and the captured plan and private settings permit sending, confirm **Ready to send** without entering limits separately for the new workspace. |

To rehearse a lost activation or a rejection, save the registration first. On the staging operator page, select that company’s draft, enter the exercise reason and choose the scenario before the owner submits. The scenario applies once to that registration revision. Let the worker run. The lost-notification case should show a reserved number until the scheduled lookup confirms its signed receipt. A normal activation should say it was confirmed by carrier notification. In the rejection case, the owner should see the SMS terms correction and be able to submit a corrected revision.

For a sole proprietor whose captured payer details conflict, the operator can void the specific simulated receipt and return the application for correction. The owner must save again, verify all three new codes and complete a fresh practice checkout. The prior receipt cannot fund submission. The void applies only to mock capture.

Actual control of the applicant’s email and phone, real payer evidence, real sole-proprietor verification, Twilio acceptance of DBAs and hosted pages, and approval and delivery for the owner's actual businesses remain external evidence for WA-05-AT04 and FL-AT-045.
